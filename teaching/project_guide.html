At a glance

You are randomly assigned 5 sites on and around the National Mall. At each one you complete two short surveys on your phone:

  • Survey A: Interview. Interview one adult passer-by (about 5 minutes).
  • Survey B: Water. Walk to the nearest place selling bottled water and record its type, a photo, the price of a bottle and its location (about 5 minutes). You do not need to buy anything.

In total: 5 completed interviews and 5 water surveys. The project is worth 15% of your grade.

Working without signal

The forms work offline once they have been opened. If you submit without signal, the form keeps your submission on the phone and sends it the next time you open the form with a connection. Before you leave a site, check that the form shows no submissions still waiting to be sent. Don't clear your browser data while any are waiting.

Moving through a form

Each form shows one question per screen. Answer it, then tap Next. You can't move on until a required question is answered, and some questions only appear for certain answers. Tap Submit on the last screen.

At each site

  1. Go to the meeting point of one of your assigned sites.
  2. Survey A, Interview: approach passers-by near the site.
    • Start a new interview form for every person you approach, even if they say no.
    • Read the script exactly as written (practice it before you go out):
      Read aloud:

      "Hi, I'm a GW student doing a 3-minute anonymous survey for my economics class about Economic Development. Would you agree to participate? I won't ask your name or take your photo, you can stop the survey at any time, and your answers are only used for learning purposes in my class."
    • If they decline or are under 18, the form says so. Record their GPS and submit, then try the next person.
    • Read exactly what the form shows. For a random half of respondents the form shows an extra line to read aloud about foreign aid; for the others it doesn't. Never give anyone the answers before the debrief at the end, and never suggest numbers.
    • Keep going until you have one completed interview. Refusals are recorded but don't count toward your 5.
    • The form ends with a GPS point. Take it while you are still at the site.
    • Full survey transcript (every question, in order)
  3. Survey B, Water: open the water form.
    • From the meeting point, walk to the nearest place selling bottled water: a vending machine, food truck, kiosk, café or shop.
    • You do not need to buy anything. Find the bottle of plain water closest to 500 ml (16.9 oz). If only ml is shown on the label, divide by 29.57 to get ounces.
    • Complete the form. It ends with a GPS point, which you take at the water seller.
    • Full survey transcript (every question, in order)
  4. Make sure both surveys have been sent, then move on to your next site.

Check what has been received. In every form, the screen after you choose a site shows which of your surveys have reached me: the interview and water survey for that site, your totals for all 5 sites, and your pilot, with the time it was last updated. A new submission shows up within a few minutes; to see it, close the form and open it again with signal. To check without filling anything in, type your GWID, choose a site, read that screen, and close the form.

Made a typo? Fill in the form again for the same site and answer “Yes” to the question about correcting a mistake. Only your latest submission for each site counts (the latest completed one, for interviews). No need to email me.

Ethics and safety

  • Adults only (18+). No children, even with a parent's permission.
  • Anonymous: never write down names, and never photograph respondents.
  • Respect “no”: thank them and move on. Anyone can stop whenever they like.
  • Don't interview classmates from ECON 2151.
  • Safety: go in daylight. You may go with a classmate, but each of you does your own interviews, and never with the same respondent.
  • Be polite and brief. You represent GW.

GW's Office of Human Research reviewed this project as a class exercise: the data are used in this class only and never published. The rules above are part of that.

Troubleshooting

My GWID isn't accepted, or no sites are listed

Type only the 8 digits after the G, in both GWID boxes. If it is still rejected, you may be missing from the class list (for example, if you added the course late). Email me your GWID and I will add you, usually within a day.

The GPS question says location is blocked

iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → turn it on, then Safari Websites → While Using the App, with Precise Location on. Reload the form.

Android: turn on Location in the quick settings. Then in Chrome: ⋮ → Settings → Site settings → Location → allow ee.kobotoolbox.org. Reload the form.

The GPS accuracy won't get to 10 m

Stand in the open, away from tall buildings and trees, and wait 20–30 seconds. Turning on Wi-Fi helps even without connecting to a network. If it stops improving, accept the best reading you get.

The camera won't open or the photo won't attach

Allow camera access. On iPhone: Settings → Apps → Safari → Camera → Allow. On Android: Chrome → ⋮ → Settings → Site settings → Camera. If the camera still won't open, take the photo with your normal camera app and choose it from your library in the form.

I have no signal

Keep going. The form saves your submission on the phone and sends it when you next open the form with a connection. Check later that nothing is still waiting to be sent.

The form says a survey hasn't been received, but I sent it

Wait a few minutes, then close the form and open it again with signal. If you sent it offline, it is still on your phone: opening the form with a connection sends it. “Only refusals so far” means every interview you sent for that site was a refusal or under-18; you still need one completed interview there. If it still isn't showing after an hour, email me.

I submitted a mistake, or submitted twice

Fill in the form again for the same site and answer “Yes” to the correcting-a-mistake question. Only your latest submission for each site counts. No need to email me.

I lost the home-screen icon

Open the form again from the links above, then add it to your home screen again. Nothing is lost.

A respondent wants to know where the facts come from

The debrief screen links a source for each fact. You can show them the screen.

I don't have a smartphone, or mine is broken

Email me as early as possible so we can arrange an alternative. Don't wait until November.
